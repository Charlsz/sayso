# 2026-10-27 judgment batch

One paired pass of the ten `judgment` prompts, made before the correctness and code-review instructions were added to `SKILL.md` and `references/code.md`. This file records what that pass showed. It is not a score for the instructions that were current when the later five-case pass was run, and it was not repeated.

- Model: Grok 4.7, inherited by the session that ran the pair.
- Settings: temperature and other sampling settings were not exposed.
- Runs per condition: 1.
- With Sayso: the session was told to read `SKILL.md` and the matching reference. That forces the skill into context. It does not measure whether a normal chat would load Sayso on its own.
- Without Sayso: the session was told not to read the repository and not to apply a skill. Tool traces were not available, so that instruction was not independently verified.

## What differed

- `preserve-voice`: Sayso kept the lowercase draft and fixed "its" plus a comma. The other answer capitalized it into "I've been working…"
- `leave-unchanged-message`: Sayso returned the message unchanged. The other answer capitalized it.
- `project-advice`: Sayso stayed on steps that used job posts and existing repos. The other answer introduced a course number, ELEC 372, and a tool name, boardflash, inside a resume sentence it said to use only once those facts were true.
- `precision-recall`: Sayso wrote that raising a threshold "typically removes some false positives and therefore increases precision." The other answer said precision does not change monotonically. Sayso's flood example used stipulated pixel counts and labeled them as an illustration.
- `get-user-review`: both answers reported that an HTTP error body can be cached as a user, and that `id` is inserted into the path without encoding. Sayso stopped there, apart from empty, `.`, and `..` path segments. The other answer also reported that two overlapping calls can both miss the cache and both call `fetch`.

The other five prompts were close. Both sides stayed formal on the professor email and the flood paragraph. Both kept the satellite summary's numbers, the two-region limit, and the missing confidence intervals. The process-and-thread answers were both short explanations. The casual meetup rewrite was casual on both sides. The answer without Sayso was the more text-like of the two.

Those differences are one run. They motivated the assertions on `precision-recall` and `get-user-review`. They do not show that the edited skill produces those results.
