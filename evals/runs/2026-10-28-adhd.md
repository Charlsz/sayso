# 2026-10-28 ADHD skill comparison

One pass of eleven prompts under three conditions: no skill, Sayso, and [ayghri/i-have-adhd](https://github.com/ayghri/i-have-adhd). The answers are in [`2026-10-28-adhd.json`](2026-10-28-adhd.json). This file says what that pass showed. It was not repeated. It is not a ranking, and it is not a result for the README or the site.

- Date: October 28, 2026.
- Model: Grok 4.7, inherited by the sessions that wrote the answers.
- Settings: temperature and other sampling settings were not exposed.
- Runs per condition: 1.
- Sayso: commit `ca7836b`. The session was told to read `SKILL.md` and any reference that file pointed to. `sayso_refs` in the JSON is what the held-prompt and action-prompt sessions named. The review and carry sessions were not asked to name a file, so those entries are null. That puts the skill in context. It does not measure whether a normal chat would load Sayso from `/sayso`.
- No skill: the session was told not to read a skill file.
- ADHD skill: the `SKILL.md` on `main` of `ayghri/i-have-adhd`, fetched that morning from the raw file. It was not pinned to a commit, and the session did not type `/i-have-adhd`.
- Sessions: the four held prompts (`leave-unchanged-message`, `homepage-ticket`, `preserve-voice`, `export-flag`) shared one session per condition. The four action prompts (`cookie-test`, `password-reset`, `redis-demo`, `dataset-email`) shared another. `get-user-review` was its own session. `carry-sister` and `carry-lab` were two turns of one session. Assertions were not sent.

The four held prompts and `get-user-review` use the `prompt` text in `evals.json`. The action prompts and the two carry prompts were written for this pass. They are stored on the JSON file because they are not cases in the suite.

`check_suite.py` scores `with_sayso` and `without_sayso` only, and only for slugs that already have objective checks. It does not grade `with_adhd`, and it does not grade the prompts that are not in `evals.json`. A passing check is not a passing reading of the answer.

## Next action

On `cookie-test`, `password-reset`, and `dataset-email`, all three answers named the fix. The ADHD answers put it in numbered steps, gave a time in minutes, and ended on one next line. Sayso and the no-skill answers said the same fix in prose.

On `cookie-test` the file was still unopened. Sayso's first line was "Open the login test and check the cookie name it expects," and it said to confirm that name before editing. The ADHD answer's first line was "Change the login test's expected cookie from `session` to `session_id`," and step 2 was that edit. The no-skill answer also said to update the test to `session_id` and run it.

On `password-reset`, all three said to read `token` from the query string. The Node 18 README line stayed a separate edit. The ADHD answer offered it as its own question: "Want that updated to 20 next?"

On `dataset-email`, none of the answers invented the owner's name. Each greeting was "Hello."

For these three, the numbered shape was the closer fit to "what should I do next."

## Voice and a finished text

`preserve-voice` asked for a grammar fix in the same lowercase voice.

- Sayso: "been working on this for a while and i finally got something that actually works. still a lot to fix but i'm happy with where it's going"
- No skill: "Been working on this for a while…" with "Still" capitalized.
- ADHD skill: "I've been working on this for a while and I finally got something that actually works. There's still a lot to fix…"

`leave-unchanged-message` was already clear. Sayso and the no-skill answer returned that text and nothing else. The ADHD answer kept the words, then added "Paste this as-is," "About 15 seconds to send," and "Next: paste it into the thread."

`carry-sister` was "running late, start without me," kept lowercase. The next turn asked for one lab sentence: centrifuged at 3000 g for 10 minutes at 4 C, with no measurement added. All three wrote that sentence, in normal case, with those three measurements and no others. Lowercase did not leak, and the ADHD answer did not turn the sentence into a checklist. This pair did not separate the conditions.

## Facts the prompt did not supply

`redis-demo` asked for a recommendation from the notes only: 12 people in one office, one stale user in staging, demo tomorrow. All three said keep the dict.

- Sayso stopped there and called that one staging occurrence a known limit of the demo.
- The no-skill answer added an instruction to overwrite that user's cache entry when the user is updated.
- The ADHD answer added "About 2 minutes to decide" and listed a Redis rewrite as a second option before saying to pick the first.

`export-flag`: all three said leave the flag off until legal reviews the column list, and all three kept staging and the two customers. The ADHD answer also said "About 5 minutes" to send legal the column list. The notes do not contain that duration. The objective check fails the Sayso answer and the no-skill answer. Both say "the export flag," and the check looks for "the flag" with nothing between those words. The answers still make the recommendation. The check is the brittle one.

`homepage-ticket`: all three stayed at about 4 seconds, the laptop, and that morning. None added a percentile, a browser, or a profiler. The ADHD answer added "About 2 minutes" and "Next: paste the title and those 3 lines into the tracker."

## get-user-review

Sayso reported three defects: an HTTP error body can be parsed and cached as the user, `id` is placed in the URL without encoding, and a second call during the awaits can miss the cache and write later. It also said an empty id requests `/api/users/` and that the snippet does not show whether `cache.set` is asynchronous. Those two points are extra. The overlapping calls are the third defect this case is for.

The no-skill review opened with "Two real bugs" and stopped after the cached HTTP error and the unencoded `id`.

The ADHD review reported those same two, gave a replacement function, "About 2 minutes," and "Next: replace `getUser` with that snippet." It did not report the overlapping calls.

## What this pass does not show

One run is not evidence that the skill caused the difference. Reading the file is not the same thing as a person typing `/sayso` or `/i-have-adhd` in a new chat. Sharing four prompts in one session can affect a later answer in that session. The ADHD skill text can change on `main` after this date. On the prompts that asked what to do next, that skill's shape matched the job. On the finished message, the lowercase note, and the review with a third bug, Sayso stayed closer to the job those prompts set.
